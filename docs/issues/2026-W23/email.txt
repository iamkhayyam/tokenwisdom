buildout has gotten ahead of itself. On the other, the breakthroughs
that don't trend: a 3D-stacked chip extending Moore's Law, a processor
that runs a thousand times faster without the waste heat, Huawei
building what the export-control map said was impossible. The market
priced the mood. The lab kept working.

* KNOWLEDGE, TRANSMUTED
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The reckoning isn't that AI is overhyped — it's that we keep measuring
the wrong thing. Market value moves on sentiment; the substrate moves on
physics. When the noise clears, what remains is what always did: who can
move electrons, or light, faster and cooler and at scale. This week the
answer started to include people the map said it wouldn't.
