Evolution

Progress in Writing Style

Measured across 5 substantial years, the prose lengthened and thickened: longer sentences, longer words, a denser register. The corpus grew up.

Average sentence length (words)
Average word length (letters)
Vocabulary richness (TTR %)

From 2013's 14-word sentences the prose stretched to a peak of 20 words in 2024, before settling back; average word length thickened from 4.3 letters to a high of 5.9 in 2025. Vocabulary richness is measured on length-normalised 1,000-word windows, so it compares fairly across years of very different size.

Methodology

Figures are computed directly from 267 published entries (2013–2026). Word counts tokenise the plain-text body of each post; reading time is Ghost's own estimate. Lexicon figures come from 1941 terms harvested from every edition's “The Less You Know” section across 74 editions. The link graph counts 1,619 curated outbound references logged over 75 weeks. Vocabulary richness uses the mean type–token ratio over consecutive 1,000-word windows to stay comparable across years. Compiled June 14, 2026.
