# Token Wisdom — Product Context

register: brand

> Token Wisdom is a weekly tech-futures publication and a growing encyclopedia of
> the AI era. The website's job is to make three years of curated writing feel like
> a body of knowledge worth living inside — and to convert that depth into
> subscribers. Design *is* the product here (it's a publication), so the homepage,
> topic pages, and long-form essays are **brand** register; the Lexicon and archive
> browsing lean **product** (tools that serve the reading).

## Product purpose

A house of essays + a weekly digest ("Pearls of Wisdom" / "Token Wisdom Week"),
plus standalone long-form ("A Closer Look", OP-EDs, "Dear ___ Letters"), and **The
Lexicon** — a living glossary of ~1,900 hand-defined terms harvested from every
edition's "The Less You Know" section. Strategy: treat the archive as a
**corpus/encyclopedia first, cadence second**. The corpus is the lead magnet; the
newsletter is the recurring promise.

## Users

- Tech-curious professionals, builders, researchers, and futurists who follow AI,
  deep tech, neuroscience, energy, and the social fallout of all three.
- Readers who are over-served by aggregators and under-served by *synthesis* — they
  want a trusted human curator and conceptual depth, not another feed.
- Secondary: newcomers arriving via search on a single term/essay who should fall
  into the corpus and subscribe.

## Brand & tone

- **Authoritative but human.** Tagline lives on "100% Authentic Humanly Chosen" —
  human curation is the differentiator in an AI-slop world. "The Newsletter of
  Record for the Future of Now."
- Intellectually serious, unafraid of dense ideas, with a literary streak (it coins
  its own vocabulary: *Constitutional Forcing, Substrate Failure, Spiralism*).
- A signature of dry wit and the occasional mystical wink (🔮), used sparingly.
- Voice we're moving toward: **technical journal of record** (see anti-references)
  while keeping the warmth and authorship that make it personal.

## Visual north star

**IEEE Spectrum** (spectrum.ieee.org) — its structural clarity, kicker-driven
hierarchy, read-time/metadata discipline, generous whitespace, and data-forward
authority. We adopt its **bones**, not its skin: this is a **hybrid** — Spectrum
structure with Token Wisdom's own signature (warm accent + a serif for long-form +
a mono technical voice) so it reads as authoritative, never as a generic tech-news
clone.

## Anti-references (what to never look like)

- Generic AI-news aggregators / SEO content farms (endless identical cards, no point
  of view).
- Cold corporate SaaS marketing (gradient hero-metric template, stock abstractions).
- Crypto-neon / dark-mode-because-cool. Clickbait. Listicle chrome.
- A literal IEEE clone (institutional blue everywhere, no personality).

## Strategic principles

1. **The corpus is the lead magnet.** Give the depth away to earn the inbox.
2. **Every surface ends in a reason to subscribe** — without nagging.
3. **The Lexicon is the moat** — defensible, search-rich, uniquely ours.
4. **Human curation is the brand.** Say it, show it, design like it's true.
5. **Browse by idea, not just by date.** Topics, terms, and connections over reverse-chron.
